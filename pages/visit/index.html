Panduan Rumus & Kebijakan Insentif KPI SCO
1. Komposisi 4 Pilar Bobot
  • Daily Visit Agen (15%): Realisasi / Target Kunjungan × 15%
  • Collection (25%): Realisasi / Target Collection × 25% (Input manual)
  • Daily Net Trx (35%): Net Trx Sah / (Baseline Okt + 50) × 35%
  • Growth Gross Trx (25%): (Nov Gross - Okt Gross) / Okt Gross × 25%
2. Skala Multiplier Insentif
  • > 120%: Dikunci maksimum 120%
  • 60% - 120%: Sesuai Total Skor murni
  • 50% - 59.9%: Berhak minimal 50%
  • < 50%: Hangus (0%)
3. Rumus Nominal Insentif
Insentif = Rp 50 × (Net Trx Daily × 31 Hari) × Multiplier
